the plan, page by page

Three pages, in order

No dashboards to figure out first. You fill in one page, then the next unlocks what it needs.

PAGE ONE

Where you're starting

Branch, semester, the skills you already have, and how many hours a week you can actually give this. No guessing on our end.

PAGE TWO

Where you stand

Your skills checked against real role requirements — not keyword matching, so "JS" and "JavaScript" count the same. Out comes a readiness score and the gaps behind it.

PAGE THREE

What to do next

A week-by-week list built only from what you're missing — small tasks, one mini-project, and internships that match what you've got today.

what's inside

Six pages that talk to each other

Change your skills on page one, and your score, gaps, and roadmap all update — nothing to re-enter twice.

Skill matching

Reads your skills the way a person would — "React" and "ReactJS" aren't treated as strangers.

Readiness score

One number per role, built from your skills, projects, and DSA practice, each weighted differently.

Weekly roadmap

Tasks, a small project, and a couple of resources each week — sized to the hours you said you have.

Internship feed

A pulled-together list from a few sources, filtered down to roles your current skills are close to.

Resume check

Paste your resume text and get a plain read on keyword match and which sections are thin.

Application tracker

Applied, OA, interview, offer — one line per application instead of a scattered spreadsheet.
